# The CAGED System

CAGED is a way of seeing the **entire fretboard** as five connected, movable
shapes. Master it and you will never feel lost above the third fret again.

## The five shapes

The name is the five open major chords every guitarist learns first:
**C, A, G, E, D**. Each open shape can slide up the neck to become a movable
barre chord for *any* root.

Going up the neck the shapes always appear in the same cycle — C, A, G, E, D,
then C again — and each one overlaps the next.

## Why it works

Every shape outlines the same chord tones — root, third, fifth — in a
different region of the neck. Because the shapes interlock, the root of one
shape sits inside the next. Learn where the roots are and you can play any
major chord in five places.

## Practising it

1. Pick one chord — say, **G major**.
2. Find all five CAGED shapes that produce a G chord.
3. Play them in order, lowest to highest.
4. Notice how the top of one shape becomes the bottom of the next.

The same five shapes also frame the major scale and the pentatonic boxes,
which is why CAGED is the backbone of position playing.

## A word of caution

CAGED is a map, not the territory. It is excellent for *organising* what you
already hear — but let your ear lead. The shapes are training wheels for the
fretboard, never a substitute for musical choices.
